# Getting started

Dacoo brings customer conversations, records, automations, reporting, and finance tools into one workspace. Start by confirming your workspace details and inviting the teammates who will handle customer activity.

## Your first setup

<Steps>
1. Open **Settings → Profile** and confirm your personal details.
2. Open **Settings → Team** to invite teammates and review their access.
3. Connect a customer channel from **Settings → Integrations**.
4. Open **Inbox** and assign your first conversation.
5. Add or review the customer in **Contacts**.
</Steps>

<Callout>Use the workspace switcher before making changes. Records, conversations, agents, and billing settings belong to the currently selected workspace.</Callout>

## Where to go next

Use Inbox for daily conversations, Contacts and Companies for customer context, Agents and Datasets for automation, and Reports for performance tracking.
