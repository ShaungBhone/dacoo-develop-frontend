# Contacts and companies

Contacts hold information about individual customers. Companies group related contacts so your team can understand the wider account relationship.

## Maintain a useful profile

Open a contact to review details, conversations, sales history, and insights. Edit fields inline and keep identifiers such as email addresses and phone numbers accurate.

## Avoid duplicate records

Search before creating a new contact. If duplicate contacts exist, use the merge action and carefully choose which values should be kept.

<Callout>Merging changes customer data and relationships. Review both records and their conversation history before confirming.</Callout>

## Custom records

Workspace owners can configure additional objects and attributes from Settings. New objects appear in the Records section of the sidebar for the whole team.
