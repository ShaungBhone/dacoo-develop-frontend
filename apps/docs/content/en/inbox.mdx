# Inbox

Inbox is the shared workspace for customer conversations from connected channels. Use filters and priority indicators to focus on the conversations that need action.

## Handle a conversation

<Steps>
1. Select a conversation from the list.
2. Review the customer panel and earlier messages before replying.
3. Assign the conversation to yourself, a teammate, or an available AI agent.
4. Write a reply or insert a saved reply, then verify it before sending.
5. Add tags or adjust priority so the next person has the right context.
</Steps>

## Human and AI handoff

Assigning an AI agent lets automation respond using its configured instructions and knowledge. Reassign the conversation to a person whenever judgment or sensitive handling is required.

<Callout>Always review the active channel and recipient before sending. A sent customer message cannot be recalled from Dacoo.</Callout>
