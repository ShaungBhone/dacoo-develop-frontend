# Finance

Finance tools show workspace balances, cards, and transaction history. Access depends on your role and the capabilities enabled for the workspace.

## Before moving funds

Confirm the source balance, destination, currency, amount, fee, and quoted exchange rate. Review the final summary before submitting.

## Cards and transactions

Use Cards to review issued cards and available controls. Use Transactions to search financial activity and open individual records for more detail.

<Callout>Financial actions may be irreversible. Verify the recipient and amount, and never share verification codes or card credentials.</Callout>
